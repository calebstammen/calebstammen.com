About me

I started coding at 13. I've been building things ever since.

I saved up for a laptop because I wanted to learn how to code. Small games, iOS apps, and robotics led to hands-on work in IT, security, and eventually full-stack software development.

Today, I lead software development and AI initiatives at Garmann Miller, while co-leading knowledge management and cybersecurity efforts. My work often comes down to connecting people, processes, and technology so information is easier to use and work gets done more effectively.

I'm optimistic about what AI can enable, but skeptical of hype. Attending NVIDIA GTC in 2025 and KA Connect in 2026 reinforced my interest in useful applications, especially how organizations learn and share what they know.
